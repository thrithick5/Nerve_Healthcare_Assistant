# Nutrition and Healthy Eating Guide

## Macronutrients

### Carbohydrates
Carbohydrates are the body's main source of energy. They are found in foods like fruits, vegetables, grains, and legumes.
- Recommended intake: 45-65% of total daily calories
- Complex carbohydrates (whole grains, vegetables) are preferred over simple sugars
- Dietary fiber (25-30 grams per day) is essential for digestive health
- Limit added sugars to less than 10% of daily calories

### Proteins
Proteins are essential for building and repairing tissues, making enzymes and hormones, and supporting immune function.
- Recommended intake: 10-35% of total daily calories
- Good sources: lean meats, fish, eggs, dairy, beans, nuts, tofu
- Complete proteins contain all essential amino acids
- Plant-based proteins (beans, lentils, nuts) are heart-healthy options

### Fats
Fats are necessary for nutrient absorption, hormone production, and brain health.
- Recommended intake: 20-35% of total daily calories
- Unsaturated fats (olive oil, avocados, nuts) are healthier choices
- Limit saturated fats to less than 10% of daily calories
- Avoid trans fats completely
- Omega-3 fatty acids (fish, flaxseed, walnuts) support heart health

## Micronutrients

### Essential Vitamins
- Vitamin A: Vision, immune function (carrots, sweet potatoes, spinach)
- Vitamin B12: Nerve function, red blood cells (meat, fish, dairy)
- Vitamin C: Immune function, skin health (citrus fruits, peppers, berries)
- Vitamin D: Bone health, immune function (sunlight, fatty fish, fortified foods)
- Vitamin K: Blood clotting, bone health (leafy greens, broccoli)

### Essential Minerals
- Calcium: Bone health (dairy, leafy greens, fortified foods)
- Iron: Oxygen transport (red meat, spinach, beans)
- Potassium: Heart function, blood pressure (bananas, potatoes, avocados)
- Zinc: Immune function, wound healing (meat, seeds, legumes)

## Hydration

### Water Intake
- General recommendation: 8-10 glasses (2-2.5 liters) per day
- Needs increase with exercise, heat, illness, or pregnancy
- Signs of dehydration: dark urine, dry mouth, fatigue, headache
- Water is the best choice for hydration
- Limit sugary drinks and excessive caffeine

### Beverages to Limit
- Sugary sodas and energy drinks
- Fruit juices with added sugar
- Excessive alcohol (1 drink/day women, 2 drinks/day men)
- High-caffeine beverages

## Healthy Eating Patterns

### Mediterranean Diet
- Rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts
- Olive oil as primary fat source
- Moderate fish and poultry
- Limited red meat
- Associated with reduced heart disease, diabetes, and cancer risk

### DASH Diet
- Designed to lower blood pressure
- High in fruits, vegetables, whole grains
- Low in sodium, red meat, added sugars
- Rich in potassium, calcium, magnesium

## Dietary Tips for Specific Conditions

### For Diabetes
- Monitor carbohydrate intake
- Choose low glycemic index foods
- Eat regular meals to maintain blood sugar
- Include fiber-rich foods
- Limit refined sugars and processed foods

### For Heart Health
- Reduce sodium to less than 2,300 mg/day
- Increase omega-3 fatty acids
- Limit saturated and trans fats
- Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables
- Choose whole grains over refined grains

### For Weight Management
- Control portion sizes
- Eat mindfully and slowly
- Include protein at every meal
- Eat plenty of fiber-rich foods
- Limit processed and high-calorie foods
